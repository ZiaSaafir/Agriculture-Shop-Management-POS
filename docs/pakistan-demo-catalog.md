(branch `shazeb palodhand bina road`) for the client walkthrough. It contains
four categories, four whole-pack/count units, three reference brands, eight

The catalog records products by complete sellable package. For example, one
`bag` is a single 50 kg bag; the system does not convert bags into kilograms.
No branch stock or opening inventory was created for this catalog or the three
additional products. A pre-existing wheat stock balance was left unchanged.
New demo products therefore are not available for POS sale until stock is
recorded through the appropriate inventory/purchasing workflow.

opening-balance ledger entries were added to the same organization and branch.
Names begin with `DEMO -`, ledger references begin with `DEMO-`, and reasons and
contact notes state that the entries are not real. Amounts are assumed PKR
examples, not actual receivables, payments, invoices, or supplier obligations.

## Market references and limitations

- [Kissan Cares: fertilizer price guide](https://kissancares.com/fertilizer-prices-pakistan)
  (market guide accessed 2026-10-08; its cited urea range was dated 2026-06-22).
  This is a market reference, not a government price order or supplier quote.
- [IR Farm: Super Gold 20 kg basmati rice seed](https://irfarm.com/products/super-gold-20kg-basmati-rice-seed-evyol)
  identifies the Evyol brand and a 20 kg pack. Availability and current price
  must be confirmed locally.
- [Kissan Ghar: Glyfast 48 SL 1,000 ml](https://www.kissanghar.pk/product-details?name=Glyfast-48%25-Sl-1000ml-Glyphosate-Ici&c=348)
  provides one Pakistan listing example for glyphosate 48% SL and a 1 L pack.
  The demo catalog intentionally uses the generic active ingredient/formulation
  rather than asserting that a specific brand is stocked or registered.
- [Powerhouse Express: TOTAL 16 L knapsack sprayer](https://powerhouseexpress.com.pk/products/total-thspp41602-knapsack-sprayer)
  identifies an example 16 L sprayer model.
- [Pakistan Bureau of Statistics: weekly SPI for week ended 2026-10-01](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/weekly-sensitive-price-indicator-spi-for-the-week-ended-on-01-10-2026/)
  is general inflation context, not an agriculture-product price list.

Except for the cited examples, the specific product ranges and modeled costs in
the table are demo assumptions, not verified current listings. Pesticide and
herbicide demo products are not application recommendations. Confirm current
Pakistan registration, original packaging, supplier authenticity, and approved
label directions before any real sale or use.
